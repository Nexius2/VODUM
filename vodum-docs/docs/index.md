# Your media servers. One place to manage them.

VODUM brings users, subscriptions, library access, collections and monitoring
together. Give your users their own portal while keeping administration in your hands.

## First time here?

1. [Install VODUM and complete the setup wizard](getting-started.md).
2. [Connect and synchronize your servers](servers-libraries.md).
3. [Configure subscriptions](subscriptions.md) and [communications](communications.md).
4. [Create a full backup](backup.md) and review [security](security.md).
5. Enable the [user portal](user-portal.md) and [media requests](media-requests.md) when ready.

!!! info "Documentation and installed versions"
    These guides follow the current development of VODUM. A feature described
    here may only reach your installation with the next update; see [What's new](whats-new.md).

VODUM is beta software. Create a full backup before each upgrade.
